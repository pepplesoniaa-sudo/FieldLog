CONCLUSION & ARCHITECTURAL DEFENSE:
Keying rate limits on client IP address alone is fundamentally weak. Attackers rotating across cheap proxy networks completely evade the limit. In production, rate limits must be compound-keyed: combining client IP, account email address, and device fingerprint hashes stored in a distributed Redis cache.
