# Transactional Email Copy: Marketing Voice vs. Agent Defaults

## 1. The Automated Agent Baseline Copy
The automated baseline email template generated during scaffolding:

> **Subject**: FieldLog - Verify your email address  
> **Body**:  
> Hello Tunde,  
> Welcome to FieldLog. Please use the verification code below to confirm your email address and complete your organisation registration.  
> **[ 849 201 ]**  
> This code will expire in 10 minutes. If you did not request this code, no further action is required and you can safely ignore this email.

---

## 2. Critique: Why Agent Copy Fails Real Customers
While the agent-generated text is grammatically correct and functional, it is sterile, generic, and misses critical commercial and psychological context:
1. **Cold & Impersonal Tone**: It sounds like an automated machine prompt rather than an enterprise platform designed for environmental site supervisors and remediation engineers.
2. **Missing Brand Orientation**: It gives zero reminder of what FieldLog does, why the supervisor signed up, or what immediate value awaits them after entering the code.
3. **No Urgency or Direct Support**: If the code fails or is delayed, the user has no direct contact link or explanation of what to do next.

---

## 3. What I Would Change for Real Enterprise Customers

If this transactional email were going to real commercial remediation clients, I would revise the copy as follows:

> **Subject**: Your FieldLog Verification Code: 849 201 (Expires in 10 mins)  
> **Preheader**: Confirm your email to begin capturing offline site daily logs.  
> **Revised Body**:  
> **Welcome to FieldLog, Tunde.**  
> You are one step away from setting up your organisation workspace and equipping your site supervisors to capture auditable, offline site entries.  
>  
> Enter this verification code to confirm your administrator account:  
> **[ 849 201 ]**  
> *(Valid for the next 10 minutes)*  
>  
> **What happens next?**  
> Once verified, you can invite your project reviewers, allocate supervisor seats, and configure your site boundary coordinates.  
>  
> *Having trouble on site? Contact our engineering support team directly at support@fieldlog.internal.*  
> *If you didn’t initiate this registration, please let us know immediately so we can protect your company domain.*

**Rationale for Revisions**:
- **Subject line visibility**: Puts the code directly in the subject line for mobile supervisors who read lock-screen notification banners without having to switch apps and open the full email.
- **Value reinforcement**: Reminds them of the core business problem solved (*offline daily logs*), reducing onboarding abandonment.
- **Actionable escalation**: Replaces passive *"ignore this email"* with active security defense guidance.
