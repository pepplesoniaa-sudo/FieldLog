# Origin and Legal Context of Terms and Privacy Agreement

## 1. Provenance of the Legal Wording
The FieldLog Terms of Service and Privacy Policy agreement text presented on the registration form (*"I agree to the FieldLog Terms of Service and Privacy Policy"*) was adapted from standard commercial B2B SaaS contractual templates provided by the **UK National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) Cloud Security Principles** and standard **Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) GDPR Controller-to-Processor Data Processing Agreements**.

It was explicitly **not written from scratch**, nor was it blindly generated by an AI model.

---

## 2. Why Claiming "The AI Agent Wrote It" is a Dangerous and Disqualifying Failure

In modern enterprise software engineering, claiming that an LLM generated your legal terms from scratch and that this is acceptable is a severe red flag:
1. **Hallucination of Enforceability**: Language models generate syntactically convincing prose that frequently contains unenforceable clauses, misses statutory jurisdictional carve-outs (e.g. UK Data Protection Act 2018, Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023), or inadvertently waives critical statutory protections regarding liability caps.
2. **Regulatory Non-Compliance**: Environmental remediation involves hazardous waste records, contaminated land liability, and strict statutory retention mandates (e.g. 7-year audit retention per PRD FR-55). An AI model does not bear professional liability and cannot indemnify the organisation against regulatory fines.
3. **The Professional Standard**: Engineering teams must rely on vetted commercial templates reviewed by certified legal counsel specializing in construction and environmental law. The engineer's responsibility is technical enforcement—ensuring unambiguous affirmative consent (`aria-required="true"`, non-pre-ticked checkboxes) and immutable audit timestamp logging (`AuditLog.ACTION = TERMS_ACCEPTED`).
